# Customer Service Summary: Q4 2025

*Prepared by Jordan Leclerc*

## Volume

| Month | Tickets | Average first response |
|---|---|---|
| October | 283 | 3.4 hours |
| November | 626 | 6.2 hours |
| December | 490 | 4.5 hours |

## Top topics

1. **Fissile availability (19% in November):** "When is Fissile back?" and "Will Fissile be in the Black Friday sale?" Several customers said they clicked an ad for Fissile and landed on a sold-out page. We set up a back-in-stock waitlist on November 13. It reached 700 sign-ups.
2. **Should I wait for snow? (October):** customers asking whether to buy skis now or wait. Our answer: buy avalanche safety gear and take your course now; hardgoods can wait.
3. **Black Friday orders and shipping times (November and December)**
4. **Avalanche safety kit bundles:** questions about which probe length and shovel to pair with Signal Hill.
5. **Returns and exchanges**

## Notable

- November response times doubled because of Black Friday and Fissile questions. We brought in a temporary CS helper from November 24 to December 12.
- Seven customers asked for price matching on Fissile when it came back in stock after Black Friday. We offered a free Spud Valley sock pack instead of a discount.

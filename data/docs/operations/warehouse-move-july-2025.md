# Warehouse Move: July 2025

*Owner: Dana MacLeod*

## Summary

Tephra moved from our original 3,200 square foot unit to an 8,500 square foot unit in the Pemberton industrial park.

## Timeline

- **July 10:** packing begins. Site banner announces a shipping pause.
- **July 14 to 15:** move days. No orders shipped.
- **July 16:** new warehouse open. All backlog orders shipped the same day.

## Why we moved

- We ran out of space during the 2024 holiday season and had to rent a temporary storage container.
- The new unit has a loading dock, so freight deliveries no longer need a forklift rental.
- Office space for the whole team in the same building.

## Things to know

- New address for returns: Unit 4, Pemberton industrial park. The returns page was updated July 16.
- Local pickup is now available Monday to Friday, 10 am to 4 pm.
- Shipping cutoff for same-day dispatch moved from 1 pm to 2 pm.

## Impact

- 121 orders were delayed by one to two days.
- 6 customer service tickets about the delay. No cancellations.

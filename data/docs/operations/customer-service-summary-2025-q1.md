# Customer Service Summary: Q1 2025

*Prepared by Jordan Leclerc*

## Volume

| Month | Tickets | Average first response |
|---|---|---|
| January | 234 | 3.1 hours |
| February | 219 | 2.8 hours |
| March | 211 | 2.9 hours |

## Top topics

1. **Sizing questions (31%):** mostly Plinth boot sizing and Keyhole base layer fit.
2. **Order status (24%):** normal levels.
3. **Mackenzie airbag pack (12%):** questions about flying with the airbag canister and canister refills. We added an FAQ on February 20.
4. **Returns and exchanges (11%)**
5. **Product advice (10%):** "Matier or Rohr?" is the most common question.

## Notable

- The Basalt theme launch on February 11 caused a small number of tickets about the new cart drawer (9 tickets in the first week). Fixed with a small layout tweak.
- No tickets mentioned retargeting ads or seeing too many ads.

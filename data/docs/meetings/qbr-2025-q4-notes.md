## Discussion

### October
- Low snow was the main cause of October's 18% drop. The first major storm cycle didn't arrive until November 24.
- Avalanche safety gear grew 14% in October even as hardgoods fell about 30%.

### Fissile stockout
- Fissile sold out November 12 because the 2025/26 shipment slipped from October 30 to December 12.
- Dana's stockout alert went to inventory-alerts, which did not include marketing or Tarn. The Fissile prospecting campaign kept running until November 21 and spent $8,400 sending people to a sold-out page.
- Black Friday was rebuilt around Mackenzie and Matier at short notice.
- Fixed: marketing@ and Tarn are now on inventory-alerts.

### December
- December came in 13% below November. That's expected after Black Friday. Against last December it was up 9%.

### Measurement in 2025
Four measurement or attribution problems this year:
1. February: custom audience script removed in the theme migration
2. May: GA4 consent mode change lowered GA4 session counts (reporting only, no revenue impact)
3. June to August: Performance Max taking credit for branded search
4. September to October: Meta Conversions API double counting

## 2026 priorities (high level)

1. Monthly tracking health check, run by Jordan with Theo
2. Inventory alerts wired into ad campaigns, so sold-out products pause automatically
3. Build a reporting view that compares platform purchases with Shopify orders and blended CAC every week. Every 2025 measurement problem showed up as a gap between platform numbers and Shopify.

## Q2 results

- July month to date: tracking flat on July 2024

## Discussion

### End-of-Season Sale
- Running May 12 to August 31: 25% off, deepening to 40% on July 1.
- Discounted hardgoods are over half of summer revenue, which lifted AOV to about $230.
- 150 Fissile boards are held back for September and October.

### May "drop"
- May was 50% below April. This is the normal end of spring season; May 2024 had the same pattern. May 2025 was up 6% on last year.
- The GA4 consent mode change on May 6 made GA4 sessions look worse than reality. Agreed: Shopify is the source of truth for revenue and orders.

### Performance Max restructure
- Since June 2, most Google budget sits in Performance Max. Tarn reported PMax CPA of $29 in June (from $68 in May) and ROAS of 7.8 (from 3.6).
- Priya asked: if PMax CPA more than halved, why did blended CAC go up 41% and new customers go down? And why is revenue flat on last year while spend is up 18%?
- Jordan pointed out that total Google platform revenue in June ($41.4K) was lower than May ($59.9K).
- Brand Search spend was cut from about $2,600 to $700. Branded search revenue fell to $14,700 in June, from $25,700 in June 2024.
- Theory: PMax is serving on branded searches and taking credit for people who were already looking for Tephra, many of them returning customers. It isn't finding new customers.
- Marcus agreed this is likely and will pull PMax search term insights.

- Completed July 14 to 15. Two days of no shipping, no lasting impact.

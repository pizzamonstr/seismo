# Q1 2025 Quarterly Business Review

**Date:** April 10, 2025
**Attendees:** Ingrid Halvorsen, Rafe Okafor, Priya Sandhu, Jordan Leclerc, Dana MacLeod, Marcus Webb and Sofia Kim (Tarn Digital)
**Notes by:** Priya Sandhu

## Q1 results

- Revenue: $1.17M, +8% on Q1 2024, 4% below plan
- January on plan, February 2% under, March 10% under
- Paid media spend: $229K
- New customers: 2,015 at a blended CAC of $113 (Q1 2024: 1,912 at $110)
- March blended CAC was $122, up from $104 in March 2024; new customers in March fell 8% year over year

## What went well

- Mackenzie airbag pack launch (February 3). Strong retargeting results in February.
- Basalt theme: mobile page speed nearly doubled. Mobile conversion rate held steady despite the March issues.
- Matier "Deep Days" creative was the best prospecting ad of the quarter.

## What went wrong

### Custom audience script removed in the theme migration
- The Basalt migration on February 11 removed the script that fed our two best Meta retargeting audiences (AG-30 and ENG-60).
- Nobody noticed for five weeks. Priya spotted it on March 18; Theo fixed it March 20.
- Meta retargeting CPA rose from $65 in February to $156 in March, and retargeting ROAS fell from 4.8 to 1.9.
- Tarn initially read the decline as creative fatigue, because frequency was high. In hindsight, frequency was high because the audiences were shrinking.
- **Action:** Theo has added the custom pixel to the tracking spec (v2.1). Any theme change must now be checked against the spec before launch.

### TikTok test
- Ran January 6 to March 31 at $4,500 per month, $13,500 total.
- Average ROAS 1.3 and a $241 CPA, against the $140 prospecting CPA target.
- **Decision:** do not continue TikTok in 2025. Revisit in 2026 if the platform's shopping features improve.

## Q2 priorities

2. Spring touring push in April (Rohr, Cerise, Meadows) with existing products. New products wait for the September 15 season launch.
3. End-of-Season Sale from May 12 to August 31 to clear last season's inventory.
4. Plan summer budget structure (Tarn to propose).

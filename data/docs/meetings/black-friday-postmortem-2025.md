# Black Friday 2025 Postmortem

**Date:** December 3, 2025
**Attendees:** Priya Sandhu, Jordan Leclerc, Dana MacLeod, Rafe Okafor, Marcus Webb (Tarn Digital)
**Notes by:** Jordan Leclerc

## Results

- Black Friday weekend (November 28 to December 1, 2025) revenue was up 9% on the same weekend in 2024.
- Top sellers: Mackenzie airbag pack, Matier ski, Keyhole base layer, Signal Hill transceiver.
- The November 24 storm cycle lifted traffic in the days before Black Friday. Sessions were up about 70% week over week.

## What went well

- Plan B came together in six days.
- Pylon, our new freeride splitboard, sold out its remaining 41 units at full price over the weekend.
- The "first storm" email on November 24 was our best email of the year by revenue.
- Mackenzie at 15% off sold through 38% of its remaining stock.

## What went wrong

### Fissile stockout
- Original plan (promo calendar, October 20): Fissile 15% off as the headline offer.
- The Fissile restock slipped from October 30 to December 12 because of a factory production delay. Dana knew on October 24.
- Fissile sold out November 12. Dana's alert went to inventory-alerts (Dana, Jordan, Ingrid). Priya and Tarn weren't on that list.
- Jordan posted in #marketing on November 12, but nobody told Tarn directly.
- "Fissile Splitboard: Prospecting" kept running until November 21. It spent $8,400 sending people to a sold-out page.

### Why it matters
- Fissile sold 106 units in November 2024. In November 2025 it sold 67, all between November 1 and 12.
- We had no hero hardgoods product for Black Friday in a year when hardgoods were already weak because of low snow.

## Changes made

1. marketing@ and Tarn Digital added to inventory-alerts (done November 21).
2. Theo added sold-out badges on product cards (November 14).
3. Dana will flag any supplier delay on a promo product to Priya directly, not only to the inventory list.
4. For 2026: look into automatically pausing ads when a product sells out.

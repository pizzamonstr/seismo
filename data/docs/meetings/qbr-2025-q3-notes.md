## Discussion

### PMax brand cannibalization (resolved)
- Search term review found about 58% of PMax conversions in June and July were on brand terms.
- Brand exclusions added and brand Search budget restored on August 5. August PMax CPA settled at $51 (ROAS 4.6), and blended CAC came back to $40, in line with August 2024 ($39).
- Lesson: when one campaign's CPA drops after a restructure, check blended CAC and new customers. If they don't improve, the campaign is taking credit for sales that would have happened anyway.

### Meta Conversions API double counting
- September 9 gateway change: browser and server events used different event IDs, so Meta counted most purchases twice.
- Meta reported a $50 CPA and 5.4 ROAS for September. Restated estimate is about $90 and 3.0.
- The warning sign was there: Meta CPA halved in September, but blended CAC only improved 6% on last year ($119 vs $127).
- We scaled Meta 35% for October based on the inflated number. October Meta spend is running at about $56,800 vs the $42,000 original plan.
- Found by Jordan on October 6, fixed by Theo October 8.
- Lesson: compare platform-reported purchases with Shopify orders every week.

### Early season
- October is tracking well below last year. There is almost no snow yet.
- Hardgoods (skis, splitboards, boots, bindings) are down sharply. Avalanche safety is up, driven by fall avalanche course season.
- Rafe: this is weather, not brand. Expect pent-up demand when the snow arrives.

### Fissile 2025/26
- Dana: factory production is running late. The October 30 arrival is at risk. Waiting on a firm date.

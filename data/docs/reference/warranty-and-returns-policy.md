# Warranty and Returns Policy

*Effective January 1, 2025.*

## Returns

- Unused items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund.
- Items must be in original packaging with tags attached.
- Skis, splitboards, and boots must not be mounted, skinned, or worn outside.
- Return shipping is free within Canada. US customers pay return shipping.

## Exchanges

- Free size exchanges on boots, base layers, gloves, and helmets within 30 days.

## Warranty

| Product type | Warranty |
|---|---|
| Skis and splitboards | 2 years against manufacturing defects |
| Boots and bindings | 2 years |
| Avalanche transceivers (Signal Hill) | 5 years |
| Airbag packs (Mackenzie) | 3 years on the airbag system, 2 years on the pack |
| Probes and shovels | Lifetime against breakage in normal use |
| Base layers and accessories | 1 year |

## Not covered

- Normal wear, edge damage, and base damage from rocks
- Damage from improper mounting
- Canister refills for the Mackenzie airbag pack

## How to make a claim

Email support@tephra.ca with your order number and photos of the problem.

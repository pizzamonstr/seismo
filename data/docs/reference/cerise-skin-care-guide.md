# Cerise Climbing Skins: Care Guide

Cerise skins are named after Cerise Creek, the classic skin track up to Keith's Hut.

## After every tour

1. Fold the skins glue to glue as soon as you take them off.
2. Store them in the skin bag, not loose in your pack.
3. At home, unfold and let them dry at room temperature. Never dry them next to a heater or fire.

## Keeping the glue sticky

- Keep the glue clean. Pine needles and dirt are the biggest enemy.
- Store skins in a cool place over summer. A fridge works well.
- If the glue gets dirty, use skin savers (cheat sheets) for storage.

## Re-gluing

- Expect to re-glue after about 3 to 4 seasons of regular use.
- We offer a re-gluing service for $60 CAD.

## Waterproofing the plush

- Apply skin wax before cold, wet days to stop snow from balling up under the skins.
- Rub the wax in with the direction of the plush, then buff it with your hand.

## Trimming

- Trim to leave about 2 mm of base edge exposed on each side.
- Use the trimming tool that comes with your skins.

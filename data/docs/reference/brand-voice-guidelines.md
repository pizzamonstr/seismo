# Tephra Brand Voice Guidelines

*Owner: Priya Sandhu. Last updated March 2025.*

## Who we sound like

A friend who has spent a lot of time in the mountains. Knows their stuff, doesn't show off, always takes safety seriously.

## Principles

1. **Plain over clever.** Say what the product does.
2. **Respect the mountains.** Never glamorize risk. No "send it" language near avalanche gear.
3. **Local, not exclusive.** We're proud of Pemberton, but we write for anyone who tours.
4. **Earned, not hyped.** Avoid "best ever," "revolutionary," "game-changing."

## Words we use

- Touring, earning turns, skin track, transition, objective
- Avalanche safety gear, avalanche training

## Words we avoid

- "Extreme," "gnarly," "fearless"
- "Prevents avalanches" or "saves lives" for any safety product

## Product names

All product names are places in and around the Pemberton valley. Always capitalize them and don't add "the" in headlines: "Meet Matier," not "Meet the Matier."

## Examples

| Instead of | Write |
|---|---|
| The ultimate powder weapon | A wide touring ski for deep days |
| Never get buried again | Helps keep you closer to the surface in an avalanche |
| Crush the uphill | Light enough for long days on the skin track |

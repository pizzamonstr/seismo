# About Tephra

*Internal brand overview. Last updated January 2025 by Priya Sandhu.*

## Who we are

Tephra makes backcountry ski touring and splitboarding gear for people who earn their turns. We design everything in Pemberton, BC, and sell only through our own store at tephra.ca.

We were founded in 2019 by Ingrid Halvorsen and Rafe Okafor. They met on a ski touring trip on the Meager massif, north of Pemberton, and spent the drive home sketching the gear they wished they had.

## Why "Tephra"

Tephra is the ash and rock a volcano throws out when it erupts. Mount Meager's last big eruption, about 2,400 years ago, spread a layer of tephra across the region. Geologists still dig into it to read the mountain's history.

We like that idea. Every layer tells you something, whether it's in the ground or in the snowpack. Our logo is drawn from the shape of the ice caves on Meager, which formed where volcanic heat melted the glacier from below.

## What we make

All of our products are named for places in and around the Pemberton valley.

- **Touring skis:** Matier (wide, for deep days) and Rohr (lightweight), each in men's and women's versions
- **Splitboards:** Fissile (all-mountain) and Pylon (freeride, new for 2025/26)
- **Boots and bindings:** Plinth touring boot, Bridge bindings (ski and splitboard versions)
- **Uphill gear:** Cerise climbing skins, Spearhead poles, Wedge ski crampons, Portage ski straps
- **Avalanche safety and packs:** Signal Hill transceiver, Nairn probe, Pebble shovel, Mackenzie airbag pack, Owl touring pack
- **Helmets and goggles:** Icecap helmet, Joffre goggles
- **Base layers:** Keyhole (midweight) and Meadows (lightweight), in men's and women's cuts
- **Accessories and tuning:** Spud Valley socks, One Mile beanie, Birkenhead gloves, Birken glove liners, Blowdown neck gaiter, Pioneer field repair kit, Anderson wax

## Our customers

Most of our customers are experienced ski tourers and splitboarders in Western Canada and the US Pacific Northwest. Around a third are newer to the backcountry and buy their first full avalanche safety kit from us, usually in the fall when they sign up for an avalanche course.

## Our seasons

- **Early season (October to December):** our biggest period. New setups, avalanche safety kits, and Black Friday all land here.
- **Mid-winter (January to February):** steady demand from people already touring.
- **Spring touring (March to May):** skins, lightweight gear, and glacier trips. Sales drop sharply once the spring melt ends the season, usually in May.
- **Summer (June to August):** our quietest months. Our End-of-Season Sale (mid-May to the end of August) clears last season's skis, splitboards, boots and bindings at a discount, so spend stays lean.
- **New season launch (mid-September):** all new products launch together, ahead of the early season.

## Where we work

Our office and warehouse are in Pemberton. We moved to a larger unit in the Pemberton industrial park in July 2025.

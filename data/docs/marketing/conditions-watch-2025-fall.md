# Conditions Watch: Fall 2025

*Priya Sandhu's running notes on snow conditions and what they mean for demand. These are informal notes, not official avalanche or weather forecasts.*

## October 3, 2025
- Barely any snow above 1,800 m. A few early dustings melted off within days.
- Last year at this time we had a solid early base at higher elevations.
- What it means: people aren't thinking about skiing yet. Expect slow hardgoods sales until snow arrives.

## October 10, 2025
- Warm and wet. Rain to high elevations.
- Avalanche course providers say fall course enrolment is strong anyway. People book courses before the season regardless of snow.
- What it means: avalanche safety kits (Signal Hill, Nairn, Pebble) should hold up. Push those.

## October 20, 2025
- Still very thin. Some early season touring on glaciers only.
- Customers emailing CS asking whether to wait on buying skis until there's snow.

## October 31, 2025
- October finished with almost no usable snowpack. Worst start to a season in the five years we've been tracking it.
- Our October sales: down 18% on last year. Hardgoods down about 30%, avalanche safety up 14%.

## November 10, 2025
- A small storm brought 20 to 30 cm up high. Not enough to tour on safely below treeline.
- Long-range models hint at a bigger pattern change around November 22 to 25.

## November 24, 2025
- Big storm cycle has started. 60 to 90 cm forecast over the next four days at higher elevations.
- Sending the "first storm" email today.
- What it means: pent-up demand should land right on top of Black Friday week.

## November 30, 2025
- Over a metre of new snow up high. Touring season has properly started.
- Traffic jumped about 70% the week of the storm.

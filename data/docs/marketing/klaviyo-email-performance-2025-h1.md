## Highlights

- **Mackenzie launch email (February 3):** best campaign of the half, $17,700 attributed revenue.
- **Spring Touring series (April):** four emails, steady results, especially Cerise skins.
- **End-of-Season Sale launch (May 12):** $10,700 attributed.

## Notes

- Email share of revenue rises in summer because paid media spend drops.
- The welcome flow was rewritten in March. Welcome flow conversion rate went from 3.1% to 3.8%.
- No changes to email tracking in H1.

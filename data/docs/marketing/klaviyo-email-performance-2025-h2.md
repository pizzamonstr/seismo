## Highlights

- **End-of-Season Sale, 40% off (July 1) and last call (August 25):** $11,900 attributed together.
- **2025/26 season launch (September 15):** $14,300 attributed, the best launch email we've sent.

- **"First storm" email (November 24):** best email of the year, $36,600 attributed revenue. Sent the morning the storm cycle began.
- **Avy Course Season bundle (October):** $15,600 attributed. Signal Hill, Nairn, and Pebble kit at 10% off.
- **Black Friday and Cyber Monday:** five emails and three SMS messages, $85,800 attributed. Headline offers were Mackenzie and Matier after Fissile sold out.
- **Fissile back in stock (December 15):** $12,900 attributed, sent to the 700 people on the Fissile back-in-stock waitlist.

## Notes

- The planned Fissile Black Friday email was cancelled on November 13 after Fissile sold out.
- Klaviyo numbers were not affected by the Meta Conversions API issue in September and October.

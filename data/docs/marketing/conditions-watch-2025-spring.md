# Conditions Watch: Spring 2025

*Priya Sandhu's running notes on snow conditions and what they mean for demand. These are informal notes, not official avalanche or weather forecasts.*

## March 3, 2025
- Deep, stable snowpack after a big February. Great touring.
- What it means: March should be strong for Matier and Mackenzie.

## March 17, 2025
- Spring conditions starting on south aspects. Corn cycles in the afternoon.
- Customers asking about lighter setups. Good time to push Rohr and Cerise skins.

## April 7, 2025
- Classic spring touring. Long days, glacier trips starting.
- Spring Touring content is performing well.

## April 28, 2025
- Snow line rising fast. Valley trailheads melting out.
- What it means: season is winding down. Expect May to be roughly half of April, same as most years.

## May 15, 2025
- Only high glacier touring left. Most people have put their skis away.
- The End-of-Season Sale (from May 12) is moving last season's skis, splitboards and boots.

# Mackenzie Airbag Pack: Launch Notes

*Author: Rafe Okafor. Date: January 20, 2025.*

## The product

- **Name:** Mackenzie, after Mount Mackenzie above Pemberton, where paragliders launch. A paraglider wing fills with air to keep its pilot up. An avalanche airbag does a similar job.
- **Price:** $1,199 CAD
- **Volume:** 32 litres
- **Weight:** 2.6 kg with canister
- **System:** compressed gas canister. Refillable at most ski shops.
- **Features:** dedicated avalanche tool pocket for the Nairn probe and Pebble shovel, ski and splitboard carry, helmet carry for the Icecap.

## Launch plan

- **Launch date:** February 3, 2025. Mackenzie was planned for our September 2024 season launch, but airbag certification testing took longer than expected. It's our only mid-season launch.
- **Campaign:** "Pull the Handle." Short videos showing the trigger, the inflation, and real-world use.
- **Launch offer:** a free Pebble shovel with every Mackenzie bought in launch week.
- **Stock at launch:** 280 units

## Who it's for

- Experienced tourers who already own a transceiver, probe, and shovel and are adding an airbag.
- Splitboarders who want a pack that carries a board well.

## Safety messaging rules

- Never say an airbag "prevents" burial or "saves lives." Say it can help keep you closer to the surface.
- Always show the full kit (transceiver, probe, shovel) in creative.
- Always encourage avalanche training.

# Fissile Splitboard 2025/26: Production Notes

*Author: Rafe Okafor. Date: June 18, 2025.*

## About Fissile

- **Name:** after Fissile Peak. In geology, fissile rock splits into thin layers, a nod to a board that splits in two.
- **Price:** $1,149 CAD
- **Best seller:** our top hardgoods product in November 2024 (106 units that month).

## 2025/26 changes

- New lighter core (paulownia and poplar) from a new core supplier. Saves about 200 g per board.
- New topsheet graphic based on the Meager ice caves.
- Bridge splitboard binding compatibility unchanged.

## Production

- **Factory:** Kestrel Board Works
- **Order:** 180 units
- **Planned production:** September 15 to October 10
- **Planned arrival at the Pemberton warehouse:** October 30
- **Risk:** the new core supplier hasn't produced for us before. Kestrel will do a first-article inspection before full production.

## Marketing timing

- Fall creative launches September 15 using carryover stock. Dana will hold back 150 carryover boards from the End-of-Season Sale to cover September and October.
- Planned as the headline Black Friday product.
- Carryover stock from 2024/25 is limited after the sale, so the new run must arrive on time to cover November.

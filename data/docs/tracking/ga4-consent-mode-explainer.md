# GA4 Consent Mode Update: What It Means for Our Reports

*From: Theo Nakamura*
*To: Tephra team and Tarn Digital*
*Date: May 7, 2025*

## What changed

On May 6, 2025, I updated our cookie banner and GA4 setup to Consent Mode v2.

## What you'll see

- **GA4 sessions will look lower.** When someone declines analytics cookies, GA4 no longer records their visit in full. Some visits are estimated by Google, some aren't counted at all.
- Before the change, GA4 captured about 92% of the sessions Shopify sees. I expect that to settle around 84%.
- **Shopify numbers don't change.** Orders, revenue, and Shopify's own session count are not affected.

## How to read reports from now on

1. Use **Shopify** for revenue, orders, and conversion rate.
2. Use **GA4** for trends and traffic sources, not exact visit counts.
3. Don't compare GA4 sessions before and after May 6 without adjusting. A drop of around 9% is the consent change, not lost traffic.

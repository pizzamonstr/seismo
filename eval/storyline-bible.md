# Tephra 2025 storyline bible (answer key)

This is the ground truth behind every document in `tephra-docs/`. Keep this file OUT of the RAG corpus. The chatbot should never see it; you use it to write and grade evaluation questions.

All companies, people, events, and numbers are fictional.

---

## 1. The company

- **Tephra** is an own-brand DTC backcountry ski touring and splitboarding brand, sold only on its Shopify store (tephra.ca).
- **HQ:** Pemberton, BC. Warehouse and office in the Pemberton industrial park (moved in July 2025).
- **Founded:** 2019 by Ingrid Halvorsen (CEO) and Rafe Okafor (Head of Product), who met ski touring the Meager massif.
- **Name:** tephra is the volcanic ash and rock thrown out in an eruption. Mount Meager's last major eruption, about 2,400 years ago, left the Bridge River tephra layer across the region.
- **Revenue:** $4.20M CAD in 2024; $4.36M CAD in 2025 (plan was $4.70M, +12%).
- **Paid media spend 2025:** $691K CAD (budget $710K). Monthly spend ranges from $10K (June and July) to $99K (November). MER 6.3 (target 6.0).
- **New customers:** 7,930 in 2025 (2024: 7,872; target 9,000). Blended CAC $87 (2024: $83; target $95 or less).

### Seasonal operating model
- **End-of-Season Sale, May 12 to August 31, 2025:** clears last season's Matier, Rohr, Fissile, Plinth, Bridge and base layers at 25% off, deepening to 40% on July 1. Summer spend stays lean (Shopping, PMax, Meta retargeting, email). Discounted hardgoods are over half of summer revenue; AOV about $230.
- **150 Fissile boards were held back** from the sale to cover September and October.
- **All new products launch on September 15, 2025** (2025/26 season): Pylon, Owl, Spearhead, Wedge, Portage, Pioneer, Anderson, Birken, Blowdown, women's Matier and Rohr. The 2025/26 Fissile run was due October 30.
- **Exception:** Mackenzie launched February 3, 2025, delayed from the September 2024 launch by airbag certification testing.

### The design principle
Every incident shows up as a **gap between platform numbers and Shopify numbers**. Platform CPA or ROAS moves one way; blended CAC and new customers move another (or don't move). Good answers notice the gap.

## 3. The incidents

### Incident 1: TikTok test ends (January to April 2025)
- Tarn ran a TikTok prospecting test January 6 to March 31, 2025, at $4,500 per month ($13,500 total).
- 56 purchases, average CPA $241 against a $140 prospecting CPA target (success threshold $175). Average ROAS 1.3.
- Q1 QBR on April 10, 2025 confirmed not to continue.
- April new customers 546, down 2% year over year; TikTok had been a source of first-time visitors.
- **Clues:** Jan to Apr agency reports, Q1 QBR notes, Slack January and February, marketing plan.

### Incident 2: Custom audience script removed in theme migration (February 11 to March 20, 2025)
- On February 11, 2025, Theo deployed the "Basalt" theme migration (v4.2) and "removed legacy scripts from theme.liquid." One was the custom audience builder firing `AvyGearView` and `Engaged60s` to Meta.
- AG-30 shrank from about 23,400 (February 1) to 5,600 (March 17). ENG-60 from about 29,700 to 6,900.
- Meta retargeting CPA rose from $65 (February) to $156 (March); retargeting purchases fell from 153 to 67; retargeting ROAS 4.8 to 1.9. Frequency 4.1. Prospecting steady.
- Blended CAC March $122 vs $104 in March 2024. New customers -8% YoY. Revenue -10% vs plan.
- **Red herring:** the March agency report blames creative fatigue (frequency 4.1). Frequency was high because the audience was shrinking.
- **Discovery:** March 18, Priya (Slack). **Fix:** March 20, Theo rebuilt it as a Shopify Customer Events custom pixel. AG-30 back to 16,200 by April 30.
- **Clues:** Q1 change log, Feb/Mar/Apr agency reports, Slack March, standup March 11, Q1 QBR, tracking spec v2.2.

### Incident 3: May drop is seasonality (May 2025)
- May revenue was 50% below April; +6% YoY. May 2024 had the same pattern.
- **Red herring:** May 6, 2025 GA4 Consent Mode v2 update. GA4 went from capturing about 92% of Shopify sessions to about 84%. GA4 sessions -25% MoM, more than orders (-37% on lower AOV). Shopify unaffected.
- **Clues:** May agency report, Q2 change log, GA4 consent explainer, Slack May, Q2 QBR.

### Incident 4: Performance Max cannibalizes branded search (June 2 to August 5, 2025)
- June 2: Tarn moved budget from Search into PMax without brand exclusions, to push End-of-Season Sale inventory through the product feed. Brand Search $2,600 to $700 per month; PMax $1,800 to $2,800.
- **What it looked like:** PMax CPA fell from $68 to $29, conversion rate 1.2% to 2.7%, ROAS 3.6 to 7.8.
- **What actually happened:** PMax served on branded searches, capturing people already looking for Tephra, many of them returning customers. Blended CAC rose to $65 (June) and $68 (July), vs $46 and $47 in 2024 (+41%). New customers fell (June 153 vs 183). Spend +18% YoY with revenue flat. Branded search revenue $14,700 (June 2025) vs $25,700 (June 2024). Total Google platform revenue fell from $59.9K (May) to $41.4K (June).
- **Discovery:** Q2 QBR, July 24. ~58% of PMax conversions were brand terms. **Fix:** August 5, brand exclusions plus brand Search restored. August CAC back to $40 (vs $39 in 2024), PMax CPA $51.
- **Clues:** Jun/Jul/Aug agency reports, account change log, Q2 and Q3 QBR notes, Slack July.

### Incident 5: Meta purchases double-counted (September 9 to October 8, 2025)
- Sept 9: CAPI moved to a server-side gateway. Browser pixel `event_id` = order ID; gateway = checkout token. No dedup.
- **What it looked like:** Meta CPA $73 (August) to $50 (September); Meta conversion rate 1.4% to 2.5%; ROAS 2.9 to 5.4; 846 Meta purchases vs 1,053 total Shopify orders.
- **The tell:** blended CAC only improved 6% YoY ($118 vs $127). Real new customers didn't jump.
- Tarn recommended scaling Meta 35% for October ($42,000 to $56,800); Priya and Ingrid approved September 30.
- **Discovery:** October 6, Jordan: Meta reported 163 purchases for October 1 to 5; Shopify had 86 Meta-UTM orders out of 239 total. **Fix:** October 8.
- Restated September: about $90 CPA, 3.0 ROAS.
- **Clues:** CAPI proposal (Aug 20), Q3 and Q4 change logs, Sep and Oct agency reports, account change log, Slack Sep and Oct, Q3 QBR, tracking spec v2.2.

### Incident 6: Low-snow early season (October to November 23, 2025)
- Revenue -18% YoY, -27% vs plan. New customers 740 vs 954. Blended CAC $123 vs $90 (also inflated by the Meta scale-up).
- Skis and splitboards -32% YoY; boots, bindings and uphill gear -31%; avalanche safety and packs +14% (avalanche course season).
- First major storm cycle November 24, 2025.
- **Red herring:** the CAPI issue overlapped early October. Tracking inflated Meta numbers, but Shopify revenue fell because of snow.
- **Clues:** conditions watch (fall), Oct agency report, standup Oct 14, Q3 and Q4 QBR notes, CS summary Q4.

### Incident 7: Fissile sells out before Black Friday (November 12 to December 15, 2025)
- Only 150 carryover Fissile boards were held back from the summer sale, so stock depended on the new run. 2025/26 Fissile run (180 units, Kestrel Board Works) delayed by a core material problem; arrival slipped from October 30 to December 12. Dana knew October 24.
- Air freight of 25 units for about $6,000 was declined.
- Stock October 28: 75 units. Sold out November 12 after 67 units sold November 1 to 12 (vs 106 in November 2024).
- Stockout alert went only to inventory-alerts (Dana, Jordan, Ingrid). "Fissile Splitboard: Prospecting" ran until November 21: $8,400 wasted. Campaign CPA $142 before the stockout, $1,050 after; conversion rate 1.2% to 0.1%.
- Black Friday rebuilt around Mackenzie and Matier. Pylon had only 41 units, too few to headline; it sold out at full price over the weekend.
- Black Friday weekend revenue +9% YoY. November revenue +4% YoY, -7% vs plan.
- Restocked December 15; campaigns relaunched December 16; waitlist 700.
- **Clues:** supplier email, inventory updates, team-and-roles (list membership), Fissile production notes, Slack Nov and Dec, standup Nov 18, Nov agency report, account change log, promo calendar, postmortem, CS summary Q4, Klaviyo H2.

### Incident 8: December looks bad next to November
- December -13% vs November (normal after Black Friday), +9% YoY.
- **Clues:** Dec agency report, Q4 QBR, Slack December.
